# 04 · Attacker-LLM automated search (black-box)

[← Back to index](../README.md)

> Use one LLM to attack another: generate a candidate prompt, read the target's response, refine, repeat — no gradients needed.

This is the family that made black-box jailbreaks cheap and repeatable. The frontier has moved from single refinement chains to **lifelong strategy libraries** that accumulate and reuse what works.

| Method | Origin | Year / venue | Mechanism | How it works (conceptual) | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PAIR — Prompt Automatic Iterative Refinement** | Patrick Chao et al. | 2023 | attacker-LLM; black-box (~20 queries) | An attacker LLM proposes a prompt, a judge scores how close the target came to complying, and the attacker revises.<br>It typically finds a working prompt in around twenty queries.<br>It produces fluent, human-readable attacks (unlike gradient suffixes).<br>Became the baseline every later automated method compares against. | [arXiv:2310.08419](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08419) |
| **TAP — Tree of Attacks with Pruning** | Anay Mehrotra et al. | 2023 | tree search; black-box | Generalises PAIR from a single chain to a **branching tree** explored with tree-of-thought reasoning.<br>Crucially it *prunes*: before spending a query on the target, it discards candidates a judge rates off-topic or unpromising.<br>Higher success with fewer target queries than PAIR.<br>Set a strong automated-attack baseline on frontier models. | [arXiv:2312.02119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02119) |
| **GPTFuzzer** | Jiahao Yu, Xingwei Lin, Xinyu Xing | 2023 | fuzzing; mutation + seeds | Brings software **fuzzing** to jailbreaks.<br>Starting from human-written jailbreak templates as seeds, it applies mutation operators (rephrase, crossover, expand, shorten).<br>A judge plus a bandit-style seed-selection strategy keeps what works and evolves it.<br>Discovers template families automatically and generalises them across questions and models. | [arXiv:2309.10253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10253) |
| **MASTERKEY** | Gelei Deng et al. | 2024 — NDSS | fine-tuned attacker | Two contributions. A **time-based probing** method reverse-engineers how a chatbot's hidden safety filter behaves (inspired by time-based blind SQL injection).<br>And it **fine-tunes an LLM** on successful jailbreaks to generate new ones automatically.<br>It adapts as vendors patch.<br>Showed defences themselves can be fingerprinted and routed around. | [arXiv:2307.08715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08715) |
| **AdvPrompter** | Anselm Paulus et al. (Meta) | 2024 | trained prompter; fast, readable | Trains a dedicated "prompter" LLM that, given a harmful instruction, instantly emits a **human-readable adversarial suffix** — no per-prompt optimisation at attack time.<br>Fixes the two weaknesses of gradient attacks: speed (ms vs. minutes) and detectability (fluent vs. gibberish).<br>Trained in a loop against the target without its gradients.<br>Bridges optimisation and attacker-LLM families. | [arXiv:2404.16873](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16873) |
| **AutoDAN-Turbo** | Xiaogeng Liu, Peiran Li et al. | 2024 (ICLR 2025 Spotlight) | attacker-LLM search | An attacking agent explores jailbreak strategies and stores reusable lessons in a **strategy library**.<br>Later attempts retrieve and adapt those strategies instead of starting from scratch.<br>It can also incorporate externally supplied strategies.<br>Its contribution is persistent strategy discovery and reuse, extending the AutoDAN line (see [Appendix C](appendix-C-aliases-and-dedup.md) for the name lineage). | [arXiv:2410.05295](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05295) |
| **AutoDAN-Reasoning** | Xiaogeng Liu, Chaowei Xiao | 2025 — arXiv | attacker-LLM search; augmentation | Spends additional inference-time compute over the AutoDAN-Turbo strategy library.<br>Compares multiple candidate prompts and explores combinations of strategies via beam search.<br>Can find useful strategy combinations missed by one sampled attempt.<br>A published AutoDAN-Turbo variant, not an independent Best-of-N discovery. | [arXiv:2510.05379](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05379) |
| **GASP — Generative Adversarial Suffix Prompter** | Advik Raj Basani, Xiao Zhang | 2024 (NeurIPS 2025) | attacker-LLM search; latent optimisation | A generative prompter produces **readable** adversarial suffixes.<br>Latent Bayesian optimisation searches a continuous representation of candidate suffixes.<br>Feedback refines the prompter while preserving coherence.<br>Searches for safety failures without target-model gradients, and without assuming effective suffixes must look like random tokens. | [arXiv:2411.14133](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14133) |
| **LLM Stinger** | Piyush Jha, Arnav Arora, Vijay Ganesh | 2024 (AAAI 2025) | attacker-LLM search; reinforcement learning | An attacker LLM learns to generate adversarial suffixes using RL.<br>Existing attacks and target feedback supply material for continued adaptation.<br>The learned generator amortises search across later questions and targets.<br>Failures arise when its learned suffix distribution reaches unsafe behaviours the defender's alignment did not cover. | [arXiv:2411.08862](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08862) |
| **MART — Multi-round Automatic Red-Teaming** | Suyu Ge et al. | 2023 (NAACL 2024) | attacker-LLM search; adversarial co-training | An adversarial model and a defender improve through repeated rounds of interaction and training.<br>The attacker learns challenging prompts while the defender learns safer responses.<br>New rounds expose weaknesses left after earlier safety updates.<br>An evolving attack-and-defence *training* process — "multi-round" here is not a multi-turn conversation jailbreak. | [arXiv:2311.07689](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07689) · [NAACL](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.107/) |
| **ASTRA** (strategy discovery/retrieval/evolution) | Xu Liu, Yan Chen, Kan Ling et al. | 2026 — ACL | attacker-LLM search | Distils reusable strategies from attacker–target interactions.<br>Maintains separate collections for effective, promising, and ineffective strategies.<br>Retrieval reuses successful ideas while recorded failures constrain repeated exploration.<br>A search-framework contribution via structured memory and strategy evolution. | [ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1843/) |
