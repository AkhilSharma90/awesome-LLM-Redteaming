# 05 · Gradient & token-level optimisation (white-box)

[← Back to index](../README.md)

> When you have the weights, optimise directly over tokens — even tokens no human would ever type.

GCG proved that alignment can be defeated by pure optimisation, and that suffixes trained on open models *transfer* to closed ones. Most of this chapter is the resulting lineage of faster, more fluent, and more transferable variants. For the readability-focused *genetic* AutoDAN, see [Chapter 07](07-evolutionary-and-quality-diversity.md); for the separate gradient-based AutoDAN, see below and [Appendix C](appendix-C-aliases-and-dedup.md).

| Method | Origin | Year / venue | Mechanism | How it works (conceptual) | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **GCG — Greedy Coordinate Gradient** | Andy Zou et al. | 2023 | white-box; universal & transferable | The landmark token-level attack.<br>It optimises an **adversarial suffix** by using gradients to greedily swap in tokens that maximise the probability of an affirmative opening.<br>Two findings shook the field: a single suffix can be *universal* across prompts, and suffixes trained on open models *transfer* to black-box commercial ones.<br>Proof that alignment can be defeated by pure optimisation. | [arXiv:2307.15043](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15043) |
| **BEAST — Beam Search-based Adversarial Attack** | Vinu Sankar Sadasivan et al. | 2024 | gradient-free; fast | Shows you don't need gradients — or much compute.<br>Uses **beam search** guided by the model's own output probabilities to assemble an adversarial prompt in about a GPU-minute.<br>Tuning beam parameters trades off speed, readability, and success.<br>Notably effective at inducing hallucinations as well as jailbreaks. | [arXiv:2402.15570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15570) |
| **AutoDAN (Zhu)** — Interpretable Gradient-Based Attacks | Sicheng Zhu, Ruiyi Zhang, Bang An et al. | 2023 — arXiv | gradient/token-level | Generates **readable** adversarial text progressively with gradient guidance.<br>Its objective balances attack effectiveness with language plausibility.<br>Readable attacks escape defences that equate suspicious input with high perplexity.<br>A **different paper** from Xiaogeng Liu's genetic AutoDAN — a genuine name collision. | [arXiv:2310.15140](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15140) |
| **AmpleGCG** | Zeyi Liao, Huan Sun | 2024 — COLM | gradient/token-level; attacker-LLM | Successful intermediate suffixes from optimisation become training examples for a **generator**.<br>The generator learns a distribution over suffixes conditioned on a request.<br>Sampling it yields multiple candidate attacks without repeating the full optimisation.<br>Amplifies GCG through learned transfer and search reuse. | [arXiv:2404.07921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07921) |
| **AmpleGCG-Plus** | Vishal Kumar, Zeyi Liao, Jaylen Jones, Huan Sun | 2024 — arXiv | gradient/token-level; attacker-LLM | Investigates training strategies for learning effective adversarial-suffix distributions.<br>Improves the generator's ability to produce useful candidates in fewer attempts.<br>The attack surface remains unusual token sequences and cross-model transfer.<br>A named AmpleGCG variant, not an unrelated family. | [arXiv:2410.22143](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.22143) |
| **COLD-Attack** | Xingang Guo, Fangxu Yu, Huan Zhang, Lianhui Qin, Bin Hu | 2024 — arXiv | gradient/token-level; constrained optimisation | Frames adversarial-prompt generation as **constrained text generation**.<br>Optimisation balances the attack objective with fluency, style, and contextual compatibility.<br>Can study adversarial rewrites and context insertions as well as suffixes.<br>Exposes why superficial fluency checks are insufficient: coherent text can still optimise against safety. | [arXiv:2402.08679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08679) |
| **I-GCG — Improved GCG** | Xiaojun Jia, Tianyu Pang, Chao Du et al. | 2024 (ICLR 2025) | gradient/token-level | Modifies the objectives and update process of GCG suffix search.<br>Explores more varied response targets and adapts how many token positions change together.<br>Initialisation transfers progress from easier optimisation problems.<br>Stronger, more efficient optimisation of an existing surface — not a new semantic category. | [arXiv:2405.21018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21018) |
| **Faster-GCG** | Xiao Li, Zhuhong Li, Qiongxiu Li et al. | 2024 — arXiv | gradient/token-level | Revisits the discrete-search design of the original GCG attack.<br>Reduces wasted computation while searching for effective suffixes.<br>Evaluates both local attack performance and transfer to other models.<br>A GCG optimisation variant: the change is the search procedure and practical cost. | [arXiv:2410.15362](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15362) |
| **MAGIC — Model Attack Gradient Index GCG** | Jiahui Li, Yongchang Hao, Haoyu Xu, Xing Wang, Yu Hong | 2024 — arXiv | gradient/token-level | Studies how token-index gradients affect GCG's optimisation efficiency.<br>Uses that information to prioritise more productive changes to a suffix.<br>Identifies an "indirect effect" that causes unnecessary optimisation work.<br>A computational improvement within the GCG lineage. | [arXiv:2412.08615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08615) |
| **PAL — Proxy-Guided Attack on LLMs** | Chawin Sitawarin, Norman Mu, David Wagner, Alexandre Araujo | 2024 — arXiv | gradient/token-level; surrogate-guided black-box | A local surrogate guides candidate generation for a target exposed only through an API.<br>Target feedback compensates for differences between surrogate and deployed model.<br>Avoids requiring the target's internal gradients.<br>Shows limiting direct access does not eliminate optimisation attacks; also introduces GCG++ and RAL as baselines. | [arXiv:2402.09674](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09674) |
| **FLRT — Fluent Student-Teacher Redteaming** | T. Ben Thompson, Michael Sklar | 2024 — arXiv | gradient/token-level; distillation | Optimisation encourages a target model to resemble an unsafe *teacher* in its outputs or internal representations.<br>Additional objectives favour fluent, nonrepetitive attack text.<br>The search permits more flexible modifications than fixed-position token replacement.<br>Exposes the weakness of treating readability as safety. | [arXiv:2407.17447](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.17447) |
