# 14 · Audio & speech attacks

[← Back to index](../README.md)

> Carry the attack in sound. End-to-end audio-language models have their own safety gap — one that text-to-speech of an existing text jailbreak does *not* adequately reproduce.

The safety weakness lives in the end-to-end audio representation, and the acoustic channel adds a second surface: signals can be made machine-interpretable yet hard for nearby people to perceive. Watch for the cross-modal **interference** pattern, where instruction-neutral audio changes enforcement on a separate text request.
